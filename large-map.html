🔗 Regional Faults

Regional faults (black lines) are mapped from subsurface seismic imagery. They are mostly not exposed at surface.

Geographic position should be interpreted as approximate given they are not exposed at surface.

For map of growth faults that have geomorphic surface expression as seen in LIDAR data, look at Shah, S.D. & Lanning-Rush, J. (2005). Principal Faults in the Houston, Texas, Metropolitan Area. U.S. Geological Survey and associated ArcGIS map of faults

💧 Wells

Purple markers with blue fill indicate wells, mostly near-surface wells drilled for water and geotechnical purposes.

Wells are titled with letters that map to cross sections in the associated GitHub repository and saved as PDFs.
